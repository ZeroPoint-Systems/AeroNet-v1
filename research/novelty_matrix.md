# Novelty Matrix

This is the defence against accidentally claiming something already demonstrated elsewhere. Do
not write "nobody has done this", instead write "the reviewed literature identified related work
in X, Y and Z. The proposed distinction is..." Every important novelty statement should eventually
carry a citation from `literature_review.md`.

For each area, answer: What is known? What is not known? Why is AeroNet not simply reproducing it?

## Confidence scale
- High, directly supported by ≥2 independent sources in `literature_review.md`.
- Medium, supported by one source or reasonable inference from adjacent work.
- Low, plausible but not yet verified; treat as a risk to close out before publication.
