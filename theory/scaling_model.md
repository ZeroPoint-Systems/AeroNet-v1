# Scaling Model

Investigates whether the phenomenon changes systematically with swarm size `N`, and whether a
normalised information quantity can explain behaviour across different swarm sizes. This may
become one of the scientifically most important parts of AeroNet.

## Questions to answer

- Does the information boundary shift, in absolute terms, as `N` increases?
- Is there a normalisation (e.g. information per agent, information per unit bandwidth) under
  which the boundary becomes approximately `N`-invariant?
- At what `N` (if any) do finite-size effects dominate?
